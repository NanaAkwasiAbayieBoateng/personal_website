About

Engineering data and AI systems from the ground up.

I am a Machine Learning Engineer with interests across data science, data engineering, machine learning engineering, and generative AI. I focus on practical solutions that connect reliable data foundations to useful analytical and AI applications.

My approach

From experimentation to enterprise value.

My work spans exploratory analysis, feature engineering, data pipelines, model development, and deployment. I enjoy translating an open-ended problem into a reproducible workflow that can be tested, maintained, and improved.

That includes classical predictive ML as well as generative AI patterns such as structured extraction, embeddings, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), with evaluation and responsible handling built into the process.

01. Diagnose

Define the user and decision, inspect data quality and coverage, and establish measurable success criteria.

02. Design

Design data models, pipeline components, feature transformations, and an appropriate analytical or AI approach.

03. Scale

Ship tested workflows with versioning, monitoring, documentation, and feedback loops for continuous improvement.
